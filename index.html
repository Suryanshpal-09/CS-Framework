Start here: study order and time

Built for making things first: building and AI come early, logic and theory run lightly alongside. Hours are lean estimates for focused study with exercises, and interview prep is left out on purpose.

 

At 48 h a week, a daily rhythm that works: 4.5 h on the current build or systems phase, 1.5 h on the always-on logic and math track, 1.5 h on the phase project, 0.5 h on Anki review. Keep one lighter day a week so the learning sticks.
